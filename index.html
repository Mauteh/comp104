Welcome to Harvey's Small Engine Repair Shop

Harvey's is a family-owned repair shop that has been keeping local lawn mowers, chainsaws, and generators running since 2005. We believe in fair prices, clear explanations, and getting your equipment back to you quickly.

Whether your mower won't start on the first cut of spring or your snowblower needs a tune-up before winter, our experienced mechanics are here to help.
